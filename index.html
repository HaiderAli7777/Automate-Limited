Their supplier and customer statements were being rebuilt in Excel every month because the standard Odoo layout did not show what the counterparty needed. Questions people ask before the first invoice.

How long does an Odoo implementation take?

It depends how much of the business is moving, but you get the date before we configure anything. The first two weeks are scoping, and the go-live date goes into the document you sign off at the end of them. We do not start building against a date nobody has agreed.

We are three versions behind. Can you still move us?

Yes. We migrate from 15, 16 and 17 up to 19 and carry the ledger with you. Master data and opening balances move under a reconciliation you can tie back to your old system.

Do we have to switch everything over in one weekend?

No. We run the old system and Odoo in parallel until the numbers agree on both sides. You switch when the trial balances match, not when the calendar says so.

Will our own team be able to edit the website afterwards?

That is the point of how we build it. Nobody should have to call us to change a price or swap a photo. Handover includes a written guide and training for whoever will be editing it.

Do we own the design files at the end?

Yes. Editable source files are handed over on completion, for the brand work and for the site. You are not tied to us for a colour change.

Can you work in Arabic?

Yes. English and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts built properly rather than mirrored as an afterthought. Ad copy and creative are produced in either language.

What happens after go-live?

A support retainer with a named consultant rather than a ticket queue, a response window agreed in writing, and a quarterly review of what your team is still doing by hand.
